## Gas Sensor Array Drift Dataset

Alexander Vergara (2012), UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5RP6W . Original dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/224/gas+sensor+array+drift+dataset . The current UCI page lists CC BY 4.0 and also preserves historical research-use wording in its original description.

Associated study: A. Vergara, S. Vembu, T. Ayhan, M. A. Ryan, M. L. Homer, and R. Huerta, “Chemical gas sensor drift compensation using classifier ensembles,” Sensors and Actuators B: Chemical, 166–167, 320–329 (2012), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.snb.2012.01.074 .

`features.npz` contains the 13,910 original 128-dimensional feature vectors and their batch/class identifiers. Batches 1–2 train the frozen classifiers; batches 3–10 supply evaluation records. Prediction CSV files are derived by this project. Source row identifiers are retained. The gas archive SHA-256 is `91e8f466f202e7a093d657673ce47311c3e90416f7df3057966058961c351fe4`.

## Condition monitoring of hydraulic systems

Nikolai Helwig, Eliseo Pignanelli, and Andreas Schütze (2015), UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CW21 . Original dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/447/condition+monitoring+of+hydraulic+systems .

`features.npz` contains four per-cycle summaries (mean, population standard deviation, minimum, maximum) for each of 17 sensor streams, plus the original condition profile. Sensor order: PS1–PS6, EPS1, FS1–FS2, TS1–TS4, VS1, CE, CP, SE. Features are computed in float32 from the public text files. Full-efficiency cooler cycles train the classifiers; cooler conditions 3 and 20 form held-out operating regimes for valve classification. The hydraulic archive SHA-256 is `24128aad2ee45eea7e6b63ebbd9992cdf25d0483a2cebefbfc13bc69079af1f2`.

## Simulated verification

Verification and audit delays are generated by this project. They are not observed service times from either source dataset. True losses are used by the simulation generator and evaluator; each monitoring rule accesses only the reference labels available under its simulated observation state.
